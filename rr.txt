  You’re right that there is a date-format issue. The effective-date columns (EFFECTIVE_FROM_DTE / EFFECTIVE_TO_DTE) in the files are in US format MM/DD/YYYY (e.g. 08/31/2026), whereas the override  
  importer currently expects the ISO format YYYY-MM-DD (e.g. 2026-08-31). So MM/DD/YYYY values are not handled by the loader today — that limitation is real.

  re-tested with your file.
